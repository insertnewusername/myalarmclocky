# September 18th: Getting started

Got started with the onshape, made the document, and followed through the CAD tutorial. Then I was thinking how they even got the cad for the display, as I thought I was just supposed to get it from the pcb or something, but I had managed to import the pcb from kicad successfully.
After around 30 mins of goofing around not knowing what I was doing, I decided to carefully read the tutorial, and it told me to download the display. Oops. Anyways after I downloaded it, everything went kinda smoothly, there was a minor error with importing the pcb, but I didn't think it was a big issue and ignored it.
I actually didn't know you could create a part studio in onshape as context, so that was really cool. Managed to follow along the tutorial making everything fairly well.
